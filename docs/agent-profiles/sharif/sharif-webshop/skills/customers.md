# Shahira Customers

Use this skill for customer search, customer context, and light segmentation reasoning.

## Focus

- Find customers and inspect customer records
- Identify useful follow-up candidates from available data
- Explain customer-related patterns in operational terms

## Rules

- Stay factual about what customer data actually exists
- Do not infer protected traits or personal details that are not present
- If customer outreach tooling is unavailable, suggest the follow-up but do not imply it was sent
